FIELD MANUAL / FALLOUT 2

Knowing the rules is an advantage.

01 / Fallout 1 levels and perks

53 selectable perks, a maximum level of 21 and a pick every 3 levels, or every 4 with Skilled. Sniper and Slayer unlock at level 18. Action Boy, Bonus Move and Quick Pockets have three ranks.

The planner always banks every unused perk pick. Spend skipped picks at later eligible levels, including multiple ranks at one level. Each game keeps its own saved build.

02 / Requirements and upgrades

Active chems count toward requirements. Set Buffout, Mentats and Psycho separately for each perk.

Lorri can improve five SPECIAL stats at Lost Hills. Chuck's fortune can raise Luck; Patrick's song can raise Charisma. Schedule these in Operations and upgrades. Access, costs, dialogue checks and travel time are up to you to plan.

03 / Fallout 1 skills

Original Fallout skill formulas are used, including the rounded-down average of two associated attributes. Skills cap at 200%; each training purchase costs 1 point, and tag skills gain 2% per point. Tag! also grants its fourth skill an immediate +20%.

Skilled gives +10% to every skill and no extra income. Per-level income is 5 + 2 × permanent IN, −5 with Gifted, +2 per Educated rank. Educated also grants 2 points immediately when selected. The bank cap is 99 when leveling.

04 / Traits and scope

Night Person replaces Fallout 2's Sex Appeal. Select daytime or night at 18:00 for its Perception and Intelligence modifiers; this planning time applies throughout the build, including skill income. Mutate! uses an explicit trait replacement.

Master Thief, Medic, Mr. Fixit and Speaker use their original Fallout bonuses. Conditional bonuses from Ghost and Survivalist and Master Trader's hidden pricing bonus do not satisfy skill requirements. Books, NPC skill training, withdrawal, addiction and exploits are not modeled.

    01 / Levels and picks

    One perk pick every 3 levels; Skilled changes the interval to 4. A perk's minimum level stays the same. You can postpone a choice, for example using the level 18 pick at level 20.

    The planner always banks every unused perk pick, as modern sfall does. Spend skipped picks at later eligible levels, including multiple ranks at one level.

    02 / Chems

    SPECIAL and skill requirements use active chem effects. Set Buffout, Mentats and Psycho separately for each perk. A perk remains after the effect wears off.

    The wiki confirms the chem rule; fallout2-ce checks current stats. Up to two doses of each chem and the SPECIAL cap of 10 are modeled. Withdrawal, addiction and save/load stacking are not modeled. Temporary Intelligence does not increase skill point income.

    03 / Skill points

    Per level: 5 + 2 × permanent IN, +5 for Skilled, −5 for Gifted, +2 per Educated rank. Educated and Gain Intelligence help from the next level. ACE upgrades apply from their specified level.

    Three tag skills receive +20% and double training gains. Each training purchase costs 1 point through 100%, then 2 / 3 / 4 / 5 / 6 points above 100 / 125 / 150 / 175 / 200%. Tag! doubles invested gains in a fourth skill without another initial +20%.

    04 / Automatic training

    The planner trains skills ahead of upcoming perks, then works toward optional skill goals. The unspent bank is capped at 99 points. A perk whose requirements are not met grants no effects to later levels.

    Calculations assume normal difficulty, training without temporary chems and a new character starting at level 1. Books, NPC training and event perk bonuses are not added automatically. The event archive is a checklist. A Zeta scan represents an explicitly selected successful outcome, not a guaranteed bonus.

    05 / Traits and scope

    Choose up to two traits. Mutate! requires an explicit replacement; changing Skilled changes future perk events. Here and Now grants a level already included in the target and does not create a second free pick at the same level.

    SPECIAL values include starting trait bonuses. The 40-point creation budget counts base stats only. Permanent upgrades and valid Gain perks appear as a separate value at the target level when it differs. Access to cities and surgery at a chosen level is up to you to plan.
